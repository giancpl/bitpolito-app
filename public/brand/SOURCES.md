# BitPolito brand assets

All SVG files in this directory were copied without modification from
`/home/giancpl/projects/bitpolito/images` on 2026-10-27, at the project owner's request.
The collection contains the blue/white BitPolito logos, cow illustrations, and shared icons.
Brand ownership remains with BitPolito. No budget documents, credentials, or private notes are included.

The home-screen PNGs in `public/icons/` are deterministic rasterizations of
`cow-icon-white.svg` on the official electric-blue background. Regenerate them
with `node scripts/generate-app-icons.mjs` (requires the development Playwright
dependency and its Chromium browser). The original vector assets remain unchanged.
